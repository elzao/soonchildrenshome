SOON CHILDREN'S HOME

Background,Founding & Establishment: Founded in 1994 by Director Lucy Karimi Mbugua in Mwiki, Kasarani, in direct response to escalating cases of child abuse and neglect in the community. Official Registration: Registered as a Children Charitable Institution (CCI) through the Department of Culture and Social Services. Family-Style Sanctuary: Built and structured to retain 35 children at a time, providing a safe, supportive, and family-oriented environment. Holistic Empowerment: Dedicated to rescuing, nurturing, and mentoring both boys and girls—transforming lives from hardship to leadership through education, Christian guidance, and life skills. Emergency Protection & Rescue: Serves as a vital temporary shelter and emergency protection center for children in need, working alongside social workers, partner homes, and legal channels.

Our Mission

"To be a sanctuary for less fortunate children, where they are showered with unconditional love, guidance, and mentorship in their spiritual, emotional, and physical lives. This is done by creating and maintaining a conducive environment for personal growth and development for all those that come through the gate of Soon Children Home."

Vision

"Nurturing and empowering principled young leaders who will make a difference in society through education, mentorship, counseling, guidance, and Christian teachings."
