Programmes, Services & Operations

Comprehensive care, infrastructure facilities, and operational frameworks designed to rescue, protect, and empower children.

Core Pillars of Service

Emergency Rescue & Protection

Providing immediate safe shelter, medical assistance, and protection for vulnerable children rescued from neglect or exploitation.

Education & Skills Training

Supporting pre-school learning and formal schooling to ensure every child builds a strong foundation for future self-reliance.

Spiritual & Character Building

Guiding children in Christian values, moral mentorship, and character development to foster responsible community leadership.

5. Key Operational Policies & Frameworks

A. Behavior Management & Counseling Policy

Grounded in helping children grow in the fear of God through professional and empathetic counseling:

  • Empathy & Active Listening: Greeting children warmly, listening actively without judgment, asking clarifying questions, and understanding their individual priority needs.
  • Expectation Setting: Under-promising and over-performing, clear guidelines, and regular monitoring.

B. Community-Based & Exit Strategy

The home prioritizes long-term community integration and child welfare:

  • Exit Options: Transitioning children out through Kinship Care, Foster Care, or Legal Adoption in full accordance with the Children's Department.
  • Community Advocacy & Support: Tackling underlying social issues in the area like alcoholism, child abuse, negligence, prostitution, joblessness, and lack of shelter.
  • Stakeholder Network: Collaborating with CBOs, NGOs, government departments, local churches, schools, hospitals, business leaders, and donors.
